# 08 - Legal Compliance Checklist (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Statutory Checklist for the Ontario Marketplace
This checklist details federal and provincial laws governing senior care marketplaces:

- [ ] **PIPEDA Compliance:**
  - [ ] Implement clear privacy policy detailing commercial collection.
  - [ ] Appoint a dedicated Data Privacy Officer.
  - [ ] Provide client access pathways to their records.
- [ ] **PHIPA Compliance (Ontario):**
  - [ ] Encrypt all Personal Health Information (PHI) in transit and at rest.
  - [ ] Implement and verify consent forms before displaying care plans.
  - [ ] Implement tamper-resistant audit logs tracking all PHI reads.
- [ ] **Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA):**
  - [ ] Ensure web portal meets WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards.
  - [ ] Provide text alternatives, keyboard navigation, and high contrast options.
- [ ] **Employment Standards Act (ESA):**
  - [ ] Design terms of service to reflect independent contractor status.
  - [ ] Avoid exclusivity clauses; allow caregivers to work on other platforms.
- [ ] **Workers' Compensation (WSIB):**
  - [ ] Mandate WSIB clearance check or commercial general liability insurance certificates.
