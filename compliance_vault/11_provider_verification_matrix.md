# 11 - Provider Verification Matrix (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Validation Checklist by Provider Category
Before transitioning a provider profile to `Active`, the following document checks must pass:

| Provider Category | Mandatory Documents | Verification Method | Expiry Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nurses (RN / RPN / NP)** | 1. CNO Registration<br>2. Professional Liability Cert<br>3. CPR Level C<br>4. Police VSS | 1. Query CNO Portal<br>2. Verify policy boundaries & limits<br>3. Inspect issuer & date<br>4. Check police watermark | 1. Annual (Dec 31)<br>2. Policy term (1 Year)<br>3. 3 Years<br>4. 1 Year |
| **PSWs** | 1. PSW Training Certificate<br>2. Commercial General Liability Cert<br>3. First Aid / CPR card<br>4. Police VSS | 1. Audit school credentials<br>2. Verify policy boundaries & limits<br>3. Inspect issuer & date<br>4. Check police watermark | 1. Permanent<br>2. 1 Year<br>3. 3 Years<br>4. 1 Year |
| **Therapists (PT / OT / SLP)** | 1. College Registration Certificate<br>2. Professional Liability Cert<br>3. Police VSS | 1. Query college registry<br>2. Verify policy boundaries & limits<br>3. Check police watermark | 1. Annual renewal date<br>2. 1 Year<br>3. 1 Year |
| **Unregulated Workers** | 1. Government Photo ID<br>2. Commercial General Liability Cert<br>3. Police Criminal Record Check | 1. Biometric verification match<br>2. Verify policy limits<br>3. Check certificate date | 1. ID expiry date<br>2. 1 Year<br>3. 1 Year |
