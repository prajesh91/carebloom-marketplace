# 12 - Marketplace SOP Library (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Scope & Application
This document compiles the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for all CareBloom caregivers executing home care visits in Ontario.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### SOP 01: Arriving at the Client's Home
- **Action:** Check-in via the mobile app geofencing before entering the driveway. Verify arrival matches scheduled visit time.
- **Ethics:** Ring doorbell, knock gently, state name and agency clearly: *"Hello, I am Sonia, your CareBloom caregiver."*

### SOP 02: Identity & Client Verification
- **Action:** Confirm client identity verbally. Cross-reference their name with the Digital Care Plan on the active screen.

### SOP 03: Home Entry & Safety Scan
- **Action:** Conduct a 1-minute visual inspection of the home entry for slip/trip hazards. Note if handrails are secure.

### SOP 04: Working Alone Safety
- **Action:** Ensure mobile phone is charged (above 30%). Memorize the emergency exit pathways of the home.

### SOP 05: Infection Prevention
- **Action:** Perform hand hygiene immediately upon entering. Sanitize hands before and after client contact, and after meal prep. Wear personal protective equipment (PPE) as dictated by the CNO/public health guidelines.

### SOP 06: Medication Reminders
- **Action:** Assist with medication compliance. Caregivers may only *remind* patients to take pre-packaged medications (blister packs or dosettes) unless licensed to *administer* (RN/RPN/NP).

### SOP 07: Professional Boundaries
- **Action:** Caregivers must not share personal problems, request money/gifts, or make social calls outside shift hours.

### SOP 08: Client Confidentiality
- **Action:** Do not discuss client care details with anyone other than registered family members on the dashboard.

### SOP 09: Shift Logging & Documentation
- **Action:** Document shift achievements, medication updates, and nutrition intakes before checking out.
