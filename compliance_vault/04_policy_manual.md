# 04 - Policy Manual (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Professional Code of Conduct
Caregivers must maintain professional standards:
* **Respect & Dignity:** Treat clients with dignity, respecting cultural and religious values.
* **Integrity:** Never accept gifts, loans, or request power of attorney.
* **Punctuality:** Arrive within 10 minutes of schedule. Report delays.

## 2. Privacy & Data Handling (PIPEDA & PHIPA)
In Ontario, caregivers interact with Personal Health Information (PHI). They must:
* **No Local Storage:** Never save client names, addresses, or medical conditions on personal devices.
* **Consent First:** Verify that the family has signed a PHIPA consent release prior to discussing conditions with third parties.
* **Report Breaches:** Instantly report any physical or digital loss of client details to CareBloom's Chief Compliance Officer.

## 3. Anti-Harassment & Non-Discrimination
CareBloom strictly enforces the *Ontario Human Rights Code*. Discrimination based on race, sex, sexual orientation, disability, age, or religion is met with a permanent platform ban.

## 4. Substance Abuse & Drug Safety
Caregivers are forbidden from possessing, consuming, or being under the influence of drugs or alcohol during visits. Regulated nurse caregivers must adhere to CNO drug-safety requirements and report any self-suspected impairments.
