# 05 - Provider Handbook (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Welcome to CareBloom
Thank you for joining Ontario's premier care marketplace. As an independent provider, you control your hours, your rates, and the care relationships you choose to build.

## 2. Managing Your Business via the Dashboard
* **Rate Controls:** You can adjust your hourly rates at any time. Keep in mind that CareBloom charges a 5% platform service fee to cover payment processing, insurances, and client acquisition.
* **Direct Deposits:** Input your transit number, institution number, and account number to enable direct payouts.

## 3. Shift Guidelines & GPS Geofencing
To ensure trust and transparency for families:
1. **Arrive at client location:** Check-in only when inside the geofenced boundary (approx. 50 meters from client address).
2. **Conduct the Shift:** Provide care as detailed in the Digital Care Plan.
3. **Daily Log:** Enter your shift notes at checkout. Flag any symptoms (e.g. falls, fever, confusion) so families stay updated.
4. **Checkout:** Ensure checkout is logged to finalize the invoice. Payouts are dispatched every Friday.
