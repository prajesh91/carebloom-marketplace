# 16 - Provider Training Curriculum (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Core Modules for Caregivers
To maintain high service standards, caregivers must review and complete four training courses:

* **Module 01: Client Handling & Transfer Safety**
  * Learn proper body mechanics for transfers (e.g. bed to wheelchair).
  * Safe walker and cane assistance rules.
* **Module 02: Infection Prevention & Control (IPAC)**
  * Hand hygiene protocols (5 moments of hand hygiene).
  * Proper sanitization of kitchen and bathroom areas.
* **Module 03: Professional Boundaries & Ethics**
  * Maintaining boundary safety, refusing financial favors.
  * Respecting client autonomy.
* **Module 04: Reporting & Symptom Tracking**
  * Learn how to write clear, objective shift notes.
  * Recognize signs of cognitive decline or acute illness.
