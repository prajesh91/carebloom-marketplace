# 02 - Compliance Manual (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Regulatory Context in Ontario
This manual governs qualifications for independent healthcare and support professionals operating on the CareBloom marketplace.

## 3. Vulnerable Sector Check (VSS) Standards
As required by the Ontario *Police Record Checks Reform Act*, all providers who work directly with vulnerable persons (seniors, individuals with cognitive impairments) must pass a Vulnerable Sector Check.
* **Validity:** Checked documents must be dated within the last 6 months at onboarding.
* **Renewal:** CareBloom enforces a mandatory annual VSS update.
