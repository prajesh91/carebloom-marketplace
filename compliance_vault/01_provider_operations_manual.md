# 01 - Provider Operations Manual (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Overview & Operational Model
CareBloom is a digital marketplace operating system connecting independent senior care professionals with families in Ontario. Caregivers on the platform operate as independent contractors, responsible for their own tax declarations, schedules, and professional liability.

## 2. Onboarding & Registration Flow
All caregivers must complete the following standard operational steps before receiving booking opportunities:
1. **Profile Registration:** Collection of legal name, contact information, service cities (Toronto, Mississauga, Oakville, Richmond Hill), and hourly rate requirements.
2. **Identity Verification:** Upload of two pieces of government-issued identification (one must be photo ID, e.g., Ontario Driver's License or Canadian Passport) matched via biometric selfie check.
3. **Police Vulnerable Sector Sector Check (VSS):** Mandatory check processed through the applicant's local police department.
4. **Credential Verification:** Verification of educational and professional registries (e.g., CNO registry check for RNs and RPNs).

## 3. Booking Guidelines & Service Delivery
* **Acceptance Timeline:** Caregivers must respond to care requests within 4 hours.
* **Visit Boundaries:** Visits must occur within the scheduled window. Check-ins and check-outs are recorded using the mobile application's GPS geofencing.
* **Incident Reports:** If a visit cannot be completed, the caregiver must report it immediately through the dashboard log.
