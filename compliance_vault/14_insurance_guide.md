# 14 - Insurance Guide (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Required Marketplace Coverage
CareBloom requires different insurance coverage layers to protect caregivers, clients, and platform stakeholders:

| Insurance Policy | Scope of Coverage | Recommended Limit | Estimated Annual Cost | Ontario Providers | Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commercial General Liability (CGL)** | Physical injury, property damage inside client home | $2,000,000 | $600 - $1,200 | Intact, Aviva, McDougall | Mandatory for PSWs and unregulated providers |
| **Professional Liability (PLI)** | Malpractice, errors, omission of care | $2,000,000 - $5,000,000 | $150 - $400 (Group rate via CNO/RNAO) | RNAO, BMS Group, CNO partnerships | Mandatory for RNs, RPNs, PTs, OTs |
| **Cyber Liability** | Platform data breaches, ransomware, loss of PHI | $2,000,000 | $1,500 - $3,000 | Lloyd's Canada, AIG | Platform level mandatory |
| **Employment Practices Liability** | Contractor misclassification lawsuits | $1,000,000 | $800 - $1,500 | Travelers, Chubb | Platform level recommended |
| **Commercial Auto** | Transporting clients in caregiver vehicles | $2,000,000 | Variable (requires OPCF 6A endorsement) | CAA, Desjardins, Co-operators | Mandatory for transportation providers |
| **WSIB / Workers' Compensation** | Workplace injuries, lost wages | Statutory clearance | 2.5% of payroll (if employees) | WSIB Ontario | Recommended/Mandatory for agency profiles |
