# 17 - Customer Safety Framework (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Safety Checks for Families
To protect families booking care on the marketplace, CareBloom implements several checkpoints:
* **Background Check Badges:** The UI displays a verification checkmark only for providers with a valid, clean Vulnerable Sector Search (VSS).
* **Verify Identity on Arrival:** The app dispatches the caregiver's profile photo and name to the family dashboard. Families can visually verify the caregiver before entry.
* **Incident Escalation Form:** A direct pipeline on the dashboard for reporting safety concerns, instantly routing alerts to admin coordinators.
