# 19 - Incident Response Playbook (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Trigger Event & Actions
In the event of a Level 3 incident (e.g. theft, suspected abuse, major injury):

```
[Incident Flagged]
        ↓
[Immediate Action: Call Emergency Services (if applicable)]
        ↓
[Isolate Data: Suspend Provider Account]
        ↓
[Notify Data: Inform CCO & Insurance Provider]
        ↓
[Fact Finding: Interview Client & Caregiver; Save GPS Logs]
        ↓
[Resolution: Complete Incident Audit Trail]
        ↓
[Remediation: Report to Regulatory Body (e.g., CNO, CMTO)]
```

## 2. Communications Protocols
* **Internal:** Notify General Counsel and Operations Lead.
* **External:** Provide factual, concise statements to the family and relevant professional colleges. Never share sensitive details or PHI without legal counsel approval.
