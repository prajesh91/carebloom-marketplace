# 13 - Emergency Response Manual (Ontario MVP)

## 1. Emergency Protocols during Home Visits
Caregivers must react instantly and structured in response to emergencies:

### Protocol 01: Unexpected Medical Emergency (Severe pain, chest pain, unresponsive)
1. **Call 911 immediately.** State client name, location, and condition.
2. **Administer basic first aid/CPR** as qualified.
3. **Notify the CareBloom Emergency coordinator** and family contact through the app.

### Protocol 02: Client Falls
1. **Do not lift the client immediately.** Lifting can cause further injury.
2. **Perform initial assessment:** Check for pain, head injury, or bleeding.
3. **If injured:** Call 911. If stable and uninjured, assist them to rise safely.
4. **Log event:** Complete incident report and submit for audit.

### Protocol 03: Aggressive Pets or Unsafe Home Environment
1. **Request pet containment:** Politely ask family/client to lock aggressive pets in another room.
2. **Exit if threatened:** If immediate physical risk exists, exit the home and report to the coordinator immediately.
