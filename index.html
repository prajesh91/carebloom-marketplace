💡 Marketplace Philosophy

We do not employ hundreds of caregivers, eliminating payroll overhead, union issues, and staffing liabilities. We build the operating system (SaaS) that provides independent PSWs, nurses, and therapists with billing tools, schedule optimization, and GPS proof-of-visit, while providing families absolute trust and choice.

🍁 Launch Market & Geography

Our launch strategy targets four high-density, affluent communities in Ontario with aging populations and high private-pay capacities:

  • Toronto: Large urban market, high concentration of medical partners.
  • Mississauga: Massive suburbs, strong commuter population.
  • Oakville: Affluent families, high average booking values.
  • Richmond Hill: Dense multi-generational households, diverse language needs.

💰 Revenue Stream Optimization

We leverage a multi-layered monetization strategy to maximize customer lifetime value (LTV) and platform stability:

  • Client Service Fee (15%): Value added to families for coordination, security, and verification.
  • Provider Platform Fee (5%): Paid by independent caregivers for matching and invoicing tools.
  • Provider OS SaaS Subscription: Premium features (e.g., automated tax reporting, optimization).
  • Enterprise Hospital Licensing: APIs for discharge planning integration.

🤖 AI-Driven Efficiency

AI acts as a multiplier to eliminate manual admin overhead:

  • Matching Engine: Predicts best caregiver fit using location, credentials, language, and ratings.
  • Note Summarization: Translates complex daily logs written by caregivers into simple summaries for family members.
  • Care Gap Detection: Flags changes in mobility or diet and suggests appropriate therapies automatically.

🔒 Canadian Privacy Regulations (PIPEDA & PHIPA)

As a senior care operating system, ensuring client and patient data security is paramount. The platform implements rigorous privacy guardrails:

✓
PHIPA Compliance (Ontario)

Client records and daily care logs are classified as Personal Health Information (PHI). We adhere to PHIPA regulations, providing audit trails for every access event.

✓
Canadian Data Residency

All sensitive database clusters are hosted exclusively within AWS Canada (Central) region to guarantee that zero client health details leave Canadian borders.

✓
End-to-End Encryption

All client communications, care plans, and financial details are encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

🛡️ Trust & Safety Framework

How we ensure CareBloom is the most trusted care solution in Ontario:

★
Upfront Vulnerable Sector Screening (VSS)

All matching providers must undergo and supply an approved VSS police background check from their regional police service before being listable on the platform.

★
Credential Verification Pipeline

PSWs and nurses must submit their college registry status (e.g., CNO registration numbers) which is validated against public registries weekly by our compliance team.

★
Dual-sided Ratings & Peer Reviews

Both families and providers rate each booking. Caregivers maintaining less than a 4.5 rating undergo performance audits, ensuring high standards.

🔒 Dynamic Consent Registry

PHIPA requires families to have explicit control over their consent. Revoking consent instantly blocks database access to all client medical profiles and records.
